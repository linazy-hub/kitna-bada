    Privacy

    Short version: no accounts, no ads, no tracking.

    What we keep

    Your streak, Daily scores, best scores per group and your Same group / Mixed choice are saved in your own browser on this device. They never leave it, and we cannot see them.

    What we don’t do

    We don’t ask you to sign up, we don’t collect your email or phone number, we don’t show ads, and we don’t use tracking or advertising cookies.

    Your player ID

    When you first open the game you make a player ID: your age, a nickname and, if you like, whether you are a boy or a girl. It is saved only on this device and never sent anywhere. Your nickname is shown only if you choose to join a challenge scoreboard. Players under 13 can watch scoreboards but cannot join them.

    Friend codes

    When a friend enters your code in the app, Google Firebase stores only the code and a random number for that phone, so each phone can use a code once and you can be given your stars. No names or contact details are sent.

    Question-set scoreboards

    If you choose to add your score to a question set’s scoreboard, the nickname you type and your score are stored with Google Firebase and shown to everyone who has that set’s link. Nothing else is sent. Please use a nickname, not your full name. Scoreboards are optional; you can always play a set without joining one.

    Other services involved

    The fonts come bundled with the game, so playing does not contact any font service. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps standard server logs (such as IP addresses) under GitHub’s own privacy statement. Links on the Credits page open other sites, each with its own privacy policy.

    Sharing

    When you share a result or challenge, only the text or image you choose goes into the app you pick. A challenge link carries the round and your score, nothing about you.

    Children

    The game is for everyone and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

    Questions about Kitna Bada?

    How does the Daily work?

    Five rounds, the same for everyone, new at midnight in your time zone. Each round pairs two things from the same group, and the five rounds come from five different groups. Your streak counts the days in a row you finished all five.

    How is the score worked out?

    We divide your guess by the true value, so guessing half and guessing double score the same. For heights and lengths that is the length; for states, lakes and parks it is the area; for stadiums it is the number of seats. Within 6% is 100. Half or double is 45. Five times off is 0. In between, the score slides smoothly on a log scale: 20% off scores about 89, a third off about 70, three times off about 25.

    Do I need an account?

    No. Your streak, past games and best scores are saved in your browser on this device. Challenge links work without signing up, for you and for the friend you send them to.

    Is there an unlimited mode?

    Yes. Unlimited has no daily cap. Play everything mixed, or pick a group like Temples, Rockets or Trains. It is the best way to practise before the Daily.

    What is measured for each thing?

    The question tells you: height for towers, statues, rockets and dams; length for vehicles, trains and most animals; elevation for mountains; and area for states, cities, lakes and parks. Some sizes, like full train lengths and lake areas that change with the seasons, are approximate, and the fact on the result says so.

    Where do the sizes come from?

    Each object links to its source, usually an encyclopedia entry, a government project page or a manufacturer’s specification. .

    Something looks wrong. Can I tell you?

    Yes, please. Sizes come from public records and published specifications, and we fix mistakes when someone spots them.
