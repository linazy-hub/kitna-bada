Privacy policy.

For the Kitna Bada? website and Android app. Short version: no accounts, no ads, no tracking, and nothing about you leaves your device.

What the game keeps

Your streak, Daily scores, best scores and game settings are saved only on your own device (in your browser, or inside the app). They are never sent to us or anyone else, and we cannot see them.

What we don’t do

We don’t ask you to sign up. We don’t collect your name, email, phone number, location, contacts or photos. We don’t show ads, and we don’t use analytics, tracking or advertising cookies.

The Android app

The app asks for no permissions and works offline. When you tap a link (for example a source on the Credits page), it opens in your phone’s browser, which has its own privacy policy.

The website

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub keeps standard server logs, such as IP addresses, under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. The fonts are part of the site, so no font service is contacted.

Sharing

When you share a result or a challenge, only the text or image you choose goes to the app you pick (such as WhatsApp). A challenge link carries the round and your score, nothing about you.

Children

Kitna Bada? is made for families and players of all ages. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

In the game, open Privacy and tap “Clear my saved data”. Uninstalling the app or clearing your browser’s site data also removes everything.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions: use the Contact page in the game.
